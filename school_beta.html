Our Legacy

Excellence in
Education & Innovation

UTech was established in 1958 as the Jamaica Institute of Technology, later gaining university status. Today, we are the Caribbean's premier technological university, producing graduates who lead in engineering, business, health sciences, and the creative industries.

Our mission: to advance knowledge, foster innovation, and serve our community through teaching, research, and service.
